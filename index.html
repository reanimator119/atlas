Terms of ServicePrivacy Policy

ATLAS Studio is a private desktop application that its owner uses to produce original animated videos and publish them to the owner's own social media accounts, including TikTok and YouTube. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Terms of Service

1. What the app does

ATLAS Studio lets its owner sign in with their own TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit and upload videos they created to that account through the TikTok Content Posting API. Every upload starts from a deliberate action by the owner in the app.

2. Who may use it

Only the owner of the app may use it, and only with accounts they own. You must follow the TikTok Terms of Service, the TikTok Community Guidelines and the TikTok Developer Terms whenever you use the app.

3. Your content

You keep all rights to the videos, titles, captions and covers you publish. You are responsible for having the rights to everything you upload, and for labelling content correctly, including AI-generated content where TikTok requires it.

4. Acceptable use

  • No spam, mass posting, or posting to accounts you do not own.
  • No content that breaks the law or TikTok policies.
  • No attempts to get around TikTok rate limits, privacy settings or review.

5. No warranty

The app is provided as is, without warranty of any kind. The developer is not liable for lost content, failed uploads or account actions taken by TikTok.

6. Ending use

You can stop using the app at any time by pressing "Disconnect" in its settings and by removing ATLAS Studio from the apps connected to your TikTok account.

7. Changes

These terms may be updated. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.

Privacy Policy

1. Data the app receives from TikTok

  • An access token and a refresh token for the connected account.
  • The account's open ID, display name and avatar, used only to show which account is connected.
  • The posting options TikTok returns for the account (for example, available privacy levels) and the status of each upload.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's account and to show upload status inside the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and it is not sold.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens and settings are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a local file outside any public repository. ATLAS Studio runs no servers of its own and keeps no copy of your data anywhere else.

4. Sharing

Data is sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official APIs, when an upload is made. Nothing is shared with any other party.

5. Retention and deletion

Pressing "Disconnect" in the app deletes the stored tokens immediately. You can also revoke access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Apps and services.

6. Children

The app is not meant for children and does not knowingly process children's data.

7. Contact

Questions about these terms or this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact channel listed on the developer's TikTok profile.
